        How typical is this?

        How the questions were chosen. The 12 questions are a fixed random sample of the 150 benchmark questions, not picked for flattering results. FlyHash does not win every one, and that is visible here. Twelve questions can't settle anything, so the paper's table (150 questions, 10 random seeds) is the evidence.

        What this does not show. It compares ranking quality only. At the same k, FlyHash's codes are larger and slower to compare than SimHash's, and an HNSW index beats both at this scale. See the paper's Limitations. The embeddings here are TF-IDF with SVD (256 dimensions), so the text matching is cruder than a modern sentence model.
